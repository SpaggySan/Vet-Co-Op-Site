Vet Co-op

Privacy

Last updated: November 10, 2026. This explains what Vet Co-op collects, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored scrambled by our sign-in provider, never readable by us).
  • Profile: your name, branch of service, location, bio, profile photo and banner photo. Other members can see these.
  • Resume: the headline, summary, military service, work history, education, certifications, awards, languages, skills and interests you enter, plus a PDF or Word file if you upload one. You choose who can see it: only you, your connections, or all members.
  • Your activity: posts, comments, messages, groups, connections, event sign-ups and reports you file.
  • Optional extras: a phone number (visible only to you), your approximate location if you allow it, and whether you've turned on personalized offers.
  • Usage counts: how often features are used (for example, how many messages are sent in a day) so we can set fair limits. These are numbers only; we don't use your message text for this.

What we do with it

  • Run the app: show your profile, deliver your messages, and suggest people you may know.
  • Keep the community safe: block abuse, handle reports, and limit spam.
  • Understand how the beta is used, so we can improve it and decide how to keep it free or sustainable.

Who can see what

  • Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. Group invitations and message requests stay hidden until you accept.
  • Online status and read receipts can be turned off in More.
  • Resume files open only inside the app for the people you chose. They can't be downloaded from the app.
  • Find people from my contacts: if you use this, your contacts' phone numbers and emails are scrambled on your phone before they are compared. Others can find you this way only if you turn on "Let contacts find me".
  • Personalized offers are off unless you turn them on. If you do, advertisers can only see audience sizes, and only when at least 10 members match. They never see who you are.

Services that help us run Vet Co-op

  • We store data with Supabase, a database and sign-in provider.
  • AI resume review (optional, and currently switched off): if you choose it, your resume content (not your name, email, phone or photo) is sent to Anthropic's Claude to write suggestions. The app asks your permission every time.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile, photos, resume and posts at any time.
  • Delete your account: More → Delete My Account permanently erases your profile, posts, messages, connections, photos and resume.
  • Questions or requests? Email kelton.spagnoli@wsu.edu.

Beta sign-up list

If you joined our beta list on the web, we keep your email and the optional answers you gave only to invite you to test and to send beta updates. Reply "remove" to any email, or write to kelton.spagnoli@wsu.edu, and we'll delete you from the list.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, access rules enforced in the database, and limits on suspicious activity. No service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

Where it works

Vet Co-op currently runs on iPhone only. An Android version is planned.

Who it's for

Vet Co-op is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
